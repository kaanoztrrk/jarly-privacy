Delete your Jarly account

A Jarly account is optional. It only exists if you created one to back up your records. Deleting it removes the account and its cloud backup.

The fastest way: in the app

  1. Open Jarly and go to Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm.

Your backup and account are deleted immediately. The records on your phone are not touched.

If you no longer have the app

Email kaanoztrrk411@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Jarly account", sent from the email address you signed up with. We will delete your account and backup within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted: your email address and sign-in details, and your cloud backup (transactions, categories, finance model and settings). Nothing is kept afterwards.

What is not affected: data stored on a phone where Jarly is still installed. To remove that, use Settings → "Erase everything", or uninstall the app.

More detail is in the Privacy Policy.
